◆ rcond

num_t openbeam::StaticSolveProblemInfo::rcond = std::numeric_limits<num_t>::quiet_NaN()

Estimate of the reciprocal condition number of the free-free stiffness matrix (a true estimate for the dense solver, a cheaper heuristic for the sparse one). Values near zero mean that results may be inaccurate.

◆ U

Eigen::Matrix<num_t, Eigen::Dynamic, 1> openbeam::StaticSolveProblemInfo::U

The full U vector, for all problem DoFs (in the order of CFiniteElementProblem::getProblemDoFs()): computed displacements at free DoFs and the prescribed ones at constrained DoFs. Nodal coordinates.
